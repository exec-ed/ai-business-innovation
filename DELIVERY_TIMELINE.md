# AI-Driven Business Innovation - One Day Masterclass

## Course Overview
**Duration**: 9:00 AM - 4:30 PM (7.5 hours including breaks)
**Audience**: Executives, strategists, innovation leaders, portfolio managers
**Style**: Strategic simulation with frameworks and investment decision-making
**Goal**: Learn to evaluate, prioritize, and invest in AI initiatives strategically

---

## Daily Schedule & Timing

### **Morning Session: AI Strategy Foundations (9:00 AM - 12:30 PM)**

#### **9:00 - 9:20 AM: Welcome & Strategic Context**
- **Activity**: Interactive icebreaker - "AI initiatives in your organization"
- **Format**: Roundtable sharing of current AI investments
- **Outcome**: Understand where participants are starting from

#### **9:20 - 9:50 AM: Framework 1 - AI Transformation Matrix**
- **Duration**: 30 minutes
- **Content**: Map AI initiatives across dimensions (process vs strategic, incremental vs transformational)
- **Format**: Mini-lecture + examples
- **Materials**: AI Transformation Matrix visual
- **Learning**: Not all AI initiatives are equal - strategic classification matters

#### **9:50 - 10:35 AM: Exercise 1 - AI Tech Radar**
- **Duration**: 45 minutes
- **Format**: Group assessment of AI capabilities
- **Activity**: Map your organization on the AI Transformation Matrix
- **Materials**: AI capability cards, tech radar template
- **Learning**: Where is your organization today? What gaps exist?

#### **10:35 - 10:50 AM: Coffee Break**

#### **10:50 - 11:20 AM: Framework 2 - Data Value Pyramid & Three Horizons**
- **Duration**: 30 minutes
- **Content**:
  - Data maturity progression (collection → autonomous operations)
  - Portfolio balancing (Horizon 1: optimize, Horizon 2: emerging, Horizon 3: transform)
- **Format**: Framework explanation with industry examples
- **Learning**: Build AI on strong data foundations, balance portfolio investments

#### **11:20 - 12:20 PM: Exercise 2 - Threat & Opportunity Mapping**
- **Duration**: 60 minutes
- **Format**: Competitive intelligence exercise
- **Activity**:
  - Map competitive threats from AI (15 min)
  - Map opportunities to use AI strategically (15 min)
  - Position on Three Horizons model (15 min)
  - Group presentations (15 min)
- **Materials**: Competitive landscape templates, horizon mapping worksheets
- **Learning**: AI as both threat and opportunity - strategic positioning matters

#### **12:20 - 12:30 PM: Morning Debrief**
- Key insights from morning exercises
- Transition to afternoon investment decisions

---

### **Lunch Break (12:30 - 1:15 PM)**

---

### **Afternoon Session: Investment Decisions (1:15 PM - 4:30 PM)**

#### **1:15 - 1:45 PM: Framework 3 - AI Investment Model**
- **Duration**: 30 minutes
- **Content**: Calculate and optimize ROI on AI investments
- **Format**: Framework walkthrough with RetailFlow example
- **Key concepts**:
  - Value categories (cost reduction, revenue growth, risk reduction, strategic positioning)
  - Investment calculation methodology
  - Portfolio optimization
- **Materials**: AI Investment Model template
- **Learning**: Move beyond "AI for AI's sake" - quantify business value

#### **1:45 - 3:15 PM: Exercise 3 - AI Investment Dragon's Den**
- **Duration**: 90 minutes
- **Format**: Investment pitch simulation
- **Setup**:
  - Divide into "Investment Committee" and "Project Teams"
  - Project teams pitch AI initiatives to committee
  - Committee evaluates using AI Investment Model
- **Structure**:
  - Form teams and assign roles (10 min)
  - Teams prepare pitches using investment model (30 min)
  - 3 pitches × 15 min each = 45 min (pitch + Q&A + committee deliberation)
  - Committee announces decisions (5 min)
- **Materials**:
  - Investment pitch templates
  - AI Investment Model worksheets
  - RetailFlow scenario variations (chatbot, inventory optimization, pricing AI)
- **Learning**: How to evaluate competing AI investments, portfolio allocation decisions

#### **3:15 - 3:30 PM: Break**

#### **3:30 - 4:00 PM: Framework 4 - Innovation Adoption & Scaling**
- **Duration**: 30 minutes
- **Content**: Innovation Adoption Framework (5-phase implementation)
- **Phases**:
  1. Knowledge building
  2. Pilot experimentation
  3. Selective deployment
  4. Scaling operations
  5. Comprehensive transformation
- **Format**: Framework overview with examples at each phase
- **Learning**: Implementation is a journey, not an event

#### **4:00 - 4:15 PM: Exercise 4 - Strategic Response Scenarios**
- **Duration**: 15 minutes (shortened if needed)
- **Format**: Quick decision scenarios
- **Activity**:
  - Scenario 1: Competitor launches AI initiative - respond?
  - Scenario 2: AI pilot underperforms - scale, pivot, or kill?
  - Scenario 3: New AI technology emerges - invest now or wait?
- **Learning**: Strategic agility and decision-making under uncertainty

#### **4:15 - 4:30 PM: Synthesis & Personal Action Planning**
- **Activity**: Individual reflection
- **Prompts**:
  - What AI initiative will you prioritize?
  - Where on the AI Transformation Matrix does it sit?
  - What horizon is it in?
  - How will you calculate ROI?
- **Materials**: Personal action plan template

## Integration with RetailFlow Simulation

RetailFlow is used differently than in Leadership-PM course:

**Strategic Context:**
- RetailFlow CEO faces strategic decision: where to invest AI budget?
- Options: Customer service chatbot, inventory optimization, dynamic pricing, fraud detection
- Each option mapped to Transformation Matrix and Three Horizons
- Committee must allocate $2M budget across initiatives

**In Exercise 3 (Dragon's Den):**
- Teams pitch different RetailFlow AI initiatives
- Investment Committee evaluates using strategic frameworks
- Realistic budget constraints force portfolio decisions

**The Difference:**
- Leadership-PM course = "How to execute the chatbot project" (operational)
- Business Innovation course = "Should we fund the chatbot vs other AI options?" (strategic)
