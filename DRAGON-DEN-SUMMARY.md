# Dragon's Den Exercise - Complete Context

## Overview

**Exercise:** Exercise 3 - AI Investment Dragon's Den  
**Time:** 2:45-3:55 PM (70 minutes)  
**Format:** Investment committee simulation  
**Core Learning:** Apply AI Investment Model to make strategic portfolio decisions  

---

## The Setup

### RetailFlow Context

**Company:** RetailFlow Inc.  
**Industry:** Omnichannel retail (online + 45 physical stores)  
**Revenue:** $180M annually (updated from mock data)  
**Employees:** ~1,200  
**Current State:** Regional retail chain facing pressure from Amazon and online competitors  

**Strategic Priority:** Defend market share, improve operational efficiency, enhance digital capabilities

---

## The Challenge

**CEO's Brief:**
> "We have $2M to invest in AI this year. Four initiatives are competing for funding. 
> I need the executive team to recommend where this money will have the most strategic impact. 
> Not everything can be funded—make the tough calls using our strategic frameworks."

**Budget Constraints:**
- Total budget: **$2M** (cannot exceed)
- Must fund at least 2 initiatives (diversification)
- Maximum for any single initiative: $1.2M
- Timeline: All must show results within 18 months

---

## The Four AI Investment Proposals

### 1. Customer Service Chatbot 💬
**Investment:** $450,000 (Year 1)  
**Ongoing:** $180,000/year (40% of initial)  
**ROI:** 92% Year 1, 180% over 3 years  

**What It Does:**
- AI-powered chatbot handles routine customer inquiries (order status, returns, store hours)
- 24/7 availability across web, mobile, SMS
- Escalates complex issues to human agents

**Business Case:**
- Current: 150K inquiries/year @ $12 each = $1.8M cost
- 60% are routine, 80% can be automated
- Saves: 72K inquiries × $12 = $864K/year
- Net Year 1: $414K (after costs)

**AI-Specific Factors:**
- ✅ Data readiness: 8/10 (2 years call transcripts, FAQ database)
- ✅ Continuous learning: Batch retraining monthly ($15K/month)
- ✅ Accuracy: 85% (acceptable for medium-risk customer service)
- ✅ Explainability: Low requirement (customer-facing but low stakes)
- ✅ Ethical risk: Low (no discrimination concerns)

**Matrix:** Optimize (Process/Incremental)  
**Horizon:** H1 (0-12 months)  
**The Case:** Safest bet, proven technology, but LOWEST ROI (92% vs 200% H1 threshold)

---

### 2. Dynamic Pricing Optimization 💰
**Investment:** $850,000 (Year 1)  
**Ongoing:** $420,000/year (50% of initial)  
**ROI:** 253% Year 1, 400%+ over 3 years  

**What It Does:**
- Real-time price adjustments based on demand, competition, inventory levels
- Competitor price monitoring and automated response
- Markdown optimization for slow-moving inventory

**Business Case:**
- Expected: 5-8% revenue increase ($9-14M on $180M base)
- Plus: 12% margin improvement through better markdowns
- Year 1 benefit: $10M revenue + $2M margin = $12M
- Net Year 1: $10.7M (after $1.27M costs)

**AI-Specific Factors:**
- ⚠️ Data readiness: 6-7/10 (only 18 months pricing history, needs 24+ months)
- ⚠️ Continuous learning: Real-time (expensive, $35K/month)
- ✅ Accuracy: 90% (tested in 10-store pilot)
- 🚨 Explainability: HIGH requirement (must explain prices to customers, regulators)
- 🚨 Ethical risk: **VERY HIGH** - Price discrimination lawsuits, fairness concerns

**Matrix:** Enhance (Strategic/Incremental)  
**Horizon:** H1-H2 (12-18 months to full value)  
**The Dilemma:** HIGHEST ROI but HIGHEST ethical risk + exceeds budget after ongoing costs

---

### 3. AI Inventory Optimization 📦
**Investment:** $1,100,000 (Year 1)  
**Ongoing:** $385,000/year (35% of initial)  
**ROI:** 264% over 3 years  

**What It Does:**
- Predictive demand forecasting for 50,000+ SKUs
- Automated purchase order recommendations
- Stock level optimization to reduce overstock/stockouts

**Business Case:**
- Current: $45M tied up in inventory, 15% overstock waste
- Reduce inventory by 20% → Free up $9M cash
- Reduce stockouts by 30% → Capture $2.5M lost sales
- Reduce waste by 40% → Save $2.7M/year

**AI-Specific Factors:**
- ⚠️ Data readiness: 7/10 (siloed inventory data needs integration)
- ✅ Continuous learning: Batch weekly retraining
- ✅ Accuracy: 82% forecast accuracy (vs 65% current manual)
- ✅ Explainability: Medium (internal use, need to trust system)
- ✅ Ethical risk: Low (operational decision, no customer impact)

**Matrix:** Revolutionize (Process/Transformational)  
**Horizon:** H1-H2 (operational transformation)  
**The Challenge:** Strong ROI but **EXCEEDS $2M budget** if funded alone (takes $1.1M of $2M)

---

### 4. Fraud Detection System 🔍
**Investment:** $650,000 (Year 1)  
**Ongoing:** $225,000/year (35% of initial)  
**ROI:** 315% over 3 years (HIGHEST)  

**What It Does:**
- Real-time transaction fraud detection
- Pattern analysis across payment methods
- Automated flagging and investigation workflow

**Business Case:**
- Current fraud loss: $1.8M/year (1% of revenue)
- Expected: Reduce fraud by 60% → Save $1.08M/year
- False positive reduction: Save $200K in investigation costs
- Year 1 benefit: $1.28M

**AI-Specific Factors:**
- 🚨 Data readiness: 5/10 (only 6 months fraud case history, **NEEDS 12+ months**)
- ⚠️ Continuous learning: Real-time (fraudsters adapt, AI must too)
- ✅ Accuracy: 95% (vendor claims, requires 99%+ for financial transactions)
- 🚨 Explainability: **VERY HIGH** (regulatory requirement, investigations, legal)
- ✅ Ethical risk: Medium (could flag certain demographics disproportionately)

**Matrix:** Optimize (Process/Incremental)  
**Horizon:** H1 (operational efficiency)  
**The Catch:** **HIGHEST ROI (315%) but needs 6-month data preparation FIRST** - Can't launch for 6 months

## The Investment Dilemma

### The Numbers Tell a Story:

| Initiative | Investment | Year 1 ROI | 3-Year ROI | Data Ready? | Ethical Risk | Can Deploy Now? |
|------------|-----------|------------|------------|-------------|--------------|-----------------|
| **Chatbot** | $450K | 92% | 180% | ✅ Yes (8/10) | ✅ Low | ✅ Yes |
| **Pricing** | $850K | 253% | 400%+ | ⚠️ Borderline (6-7/10) | 🚨 **VERY HIGH** | ⚠️ Risky |
| **Inventory** | $1.1M | 145% | 264% | ⚠️ Needs work (7/10) | ✅ Low | ⚠️ Budget issue |
| **Fraud** | $650K | 197% | 315% | 🚨 **NO (5/10)** | ⚠️ Medium | 🚨 **6-mo delay** |

### The Traditional ROI View (WRONG):
1. Fund Fraud Detection ($650K) - 315% ROI ✅
2. Fund Dynamic Pricing ($850K) - 253% ROI ✅
3. Total: $1.5M, leaving $500K ✅
4. Fund Chatbot ($450K) ✅
5. **Total budget: $1.95M** ✅

**Result:** Maximize ROI! 🎉

### The AI-Specific View (CORRECT):
1. **Fraud Detection:** Can't deploy for 6 months (data prep needed) ❌
2. **Dynamic Pricing:** Legal/ethical risk is VERY HIGH (discrimination lawsuits) ❌
3. **Inventory:** Takes $1.1M of $2M (55% of budget for one project) ⚠️
4. **Chatbot:** Low ROI but ready to deploy, low risk ✅

**Result:** The "safe" projects don't have the highest ROI!

## Committee Decision Framework

### Must Evaluate Each Proposal On:

**Traditional Criteria:**
- ✅ Strategic fit (Matrix quadrant, Horizons classification)
- ✅ Financial ROI (meets threshold for horizon?)
- ✅ Implementation feasibility (resources, timeline)

**AI-Specific Criteria:**
- ✅ Data readiness score (7+ required)
- ✅ Continuous learning plan (sustainable costs?)
- ✅ Accuracy vs. risk match (good enough for use case?)
- ✅ Explainability requirements (regulatory/business needs)
- ✅ Ethical risk assessment (discrimination, bias, legal)

### Portfolio Considerations:
- ✅ Horizon balance (70% H1, 20% H2, 10% H3)
- ✅ Risk diversification (not all high-risk)
- ✅ Strategic gaps (what capabilities are we building?)

---

## Common Committee Decisions & Rationales

### Option A: Conservative Portfolio
**Fund:** Chatbot ($450K) + Inventory Optimization ($1.1M) = $1.55M

**Rationale:**
- Both are low ethical risk
- Both have acceptable data readiness
- Covers H1 quick wins (chatbot) + H1-H2 transformation (inventory)
- Under budget, could add fraud detection prep ($200K data collection)

**Trade-off:** Lower ROI (92% + 264%) but higher certainty

---

### Option B: High ROI with Risk Mitigation
**Fund:** Dynamic Pricing ($850K) + Chatbot ($450K) = $1.3M  
**Add:** Ethics/legal review ($200K) + Bias monitoring ($150K)  
**Total:** $1.7M

**Rationale:**
- Captures highest ROI (253%)
- Mitigates ethical risk with safeguards
- Chatbot provides safe H1 win
- Leaves buffer for risk management

**Trade-off:** High ethical risk even with mitigation

---

### Option C: Data-First Strategy
**Fund:** Chatbot ($450K) immediately  
**Fund:** Fraud Detection data prep ($300K over 6 months)  
**Fund:** Inventory Optimization pilot in 10 stores ($400K)  
**Reserve:** $850K for H2 investments once data is ready  
**Total deployed Year 1:** $1.15M

**Rationale:**
- Only fund what's data-ready NOW
- Build data infrastructure for future
- Chatbot is proven, low-risk
- Pilots before full deployment
- Leaves budget for adjustments

**Trade-off:** Slower deployment, lower Year 1 returns, but lower risk

---

### Option D: All-In on Transformation
**Fund:** Inventory Optimization ($1.1M)  
**Fund:** Dynamic Pricing with full ethics review ($900K)  
**Total:** $2.0M (exactly budget)

**Rationale:**
- Maximize transformational impact
- Both are H1-H2 strategic initiatives
- Combined revenue impact: $12M+ Year 1
- Accept ethical risk as manageable

**Trade-off:** No diversification, all eggs in two baskets, high risk

---

## The "Aha!" Moment

**Traditional analysis says:** Fund Fraud Detection + Dynamic Pricing (highest ROIs)

**AI-specific analysis reveals:**
- Fraud needs 6 months data prep (can't deploy now)
- Pricing has unacceptable ethical risk (lawsuits could cost more than revenue)
- The "safest" project (Chatbot) has lowest ROI but might be smartest choice

**Key Learning:** 
> AI requires DIFFERENT evaluation than traditional IT. 
> Data readiness, ethical risks, and ongoing costs fundamentally change which projects to fund.
> The highest ROI isn't always the best AI investment.
