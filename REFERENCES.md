## Research Sources

The following research is cited in course materials and supplementary readings:

**MIT Sloan Management Review**
- Research on AI implementation challenges and the "Gen AI Divide"
- Finding: 95% of enterprise AI pilots fail to deliver measurable ROI

**MIT & McKinsey Joint Research**
- Studies on AI implementation success factors
- Key findings:
  - 77% of AI leaders have C-level sponsorship
  - 44% sponsored directly by CEO/Board
  - AI leaders achieve 3.8x performance improvement vs. bottom 50%
  - Strategic partnerships achieve 67% deployment vs. 33% internal builds

**Gartner Research**
- Cited statistic: 85% of AI projects fail
