# Exercise 2: AI-Assisted Strategic Analysis

**Duration:** 60 minutes (11:30 AM - 12:30 PM)  
**Format:** Table groups with human brainstorming + AI augmentation  
**Technology:** 1 laptop per table with ChatGPT or Claude access

---

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this exercise, you will:
1. Identify AI-driven threats and opportunities in your industry
2. Experience using AI tools as strategic thought partners
3. Practice effective prompt engineering for business insights
4. Understand AI's strengths (breadth, alternatives) and limitations (needs context)
5. Learn three powerful AI techniques: deep-dive, reverse prompting, AI debate

**Deeper objective:** Experience AI as augmentation tool so you naturally use it in afternoon exercises

---

## Exercise Flow

### Phase 1: Human Brainstorm (15 min)
Traditional strategic analysis—YOUR thinking first

### Phase 2: AI Augmentation (30 min)
Use AI to challenge assumptions, identify blind spots, generate alternatives

### Phase 3: Human Synthesis (15 min)
Compare human vs. AI insights, present key findings

---

## Phase 1: Human Brainstorm (15 minutes)

**Your task:**

Using sticky notes and the Three Horizons grid on your flipchart:

**RED sticky notes - Threats (10 min):**
- How could AI disrupt your industry?
- What could competitors do with AI that would hurt you?
- What happens if you DON'T invest in AI?

Write one threat per sticky note. Place on appropriate horizon (H1/H2/H3).

**GREEN sticky notes - Opportunities (5 min):**
- Where could AI create competitive advantage for you?
- What customer problems could AI solve?
- What new capabilities could AI enable?

Write one opportunity per sticky note. Place on appropriate horizon.

**Goal:** Generate 10-15 ideas from your collective wisdom and experience.

---

## Phase 2: AI Augmentation (30 minutes)

**Setup (BYOD - Bring Your Own Device):**
- **Each table designates one "AI Lead"** with laptop/tablet
  - This person shares their screen OR reads AI responses aloud
  - Others can follow along on their own devices if they want
  - Or work together around one device
- **AI Tool Options** (table chooses):
  - **Google Gemini** (recommended): https://gemini.google.com (free, no login)
  - **ChatGPT**: https://chat.openai.com (free tier, requires account)
  - **Claude**: https://claude.ai (free tier, requires account)
  - **Bing Chat/Copilot**: Built into Edge browser (free, no login)
- **Suggested Roles:**
  - "AI Lead": Opens AI tool, types prompts, reads responses aloud
  - "Scribe": Captures insights on sticky notes (different color)
  - Everyone: Participates in discussion and evaluates AI responses

**Important:** 
- ✅ You'll TYPE your sticky note content into the prompt (takes 2-3 min)
- ❌ No photo upload needed—manual typing is faster and more reliable
- ✅ All AI tools work equally well for this exercise
- ✅ Multiple people can have AI open—just coordinate so you're not duplicating work

**You'll use AI in three different ways:**

### Round 1: Risk Deep-Dive (10 min)

**Goal:** Have AI identify threats you missed or underestimated

**Copy this prompt template and customize with YOUR information:**

```
You are a strategic AI consultant specializing in [YOUR INDUSTRY].

Our team just brainstormed AI-related threats to our business. 
Here's what we identified:

[PASTE YOUR RED STICKY NOTES HERE - list the threats]

Please help us think deeper:

1. What AI threats are we missing or underestimating?
2. What second-order effects should we consider? 
   (e.g., "If competitors use AI for X, that enables them to do Y")
3. Which of these threats is most likely to be existential?
4. What early warning signs should we watch for?

Be specific to our industry. Challenge our assumptions. 
Use concrete examples from similar industries if relevant.
```

**Activity:**
1. Operator reads prompt aloud, group customizes it
2. Submit to AI, read response aloud
3. Group discusses: What insights are valuable? What did we miss?
4. Scribe captures 2-3 new insights on sticky notes (different color)

**Common "aha!" moments:**
- AI identifies threats from adjacent industries you didn't consider
- AI connects dots: "If they do X with AI, then they can also do Y"
- AI highlights second-order effects: "This doesn't just hurt revenue—it makes your data obsolete"

---

### Round 2: Reverse Prompting (10 min)

**Goal:** Have AI ask YOU questions to reveal opportunities you're overlooking

**This is powerful:** Instead of asking AI for answers, ask AI to ask you questions.

**Copy this prompt:**

```
You are an expert on AI business innovation. Your role is to ask ME probing questions 
to help me discover AI opportunities I'm overlooking.

About my business:
- Industry: [YOUR INDUSTRY]
- Company type: [DESCRIBE YOUR ORGANIZATION]
- Current AI maturity: [LEVEL 1-5 FROM EXERCISE 1]
- Key challenges: [TOP 2-3 BUSINESS CHALLENGES]

Your task: Ask me 5 probing questions that will reveal:
- Hidden opportunities in processes I take for granted
- Quick wins I might be missing
- Strategic blind spots in my AI thinking

ASK ONE QUESTION AT A TIME. Wait for my answer, then ask the next question.
Start with your first question now.
```

**Activity:**
1. Submit prompt
2. AI asks first question
3. Group discusses answer for 1-2 minutes
4. Spokesperson types response
5. AI asks second question based on your answer
6. Repeat for 3-5 questions

**The magic:** AI's questions often reveal opportunities hiding in plain sight.

**Example dialogue:**
- AI: "What's the most time-consuming manual decision your team makes daily?"
- You: "Probably pricing adjustments based on competitor moves"
- AI: "How long does your team take to respond to a competitor price change?"
- You: "Usually 24-48 hours... oh wait, AI could do this in real-time!"

**Scribe:** Capture the opportunities revealed by this dialogue.

---

### Round 3: AI Debate (10 min)

**Goal:** See different AI perspectives by having them debate each other

**This is advanced:** You'll see how different AIs have different "thinking styles."

**Setup:**
- Open two browser tabs/windows
- ChatGPT in one, Claude in the other (or use same AI, but give it two roles)
- Set up the debate

**Prompt for ChatGPT:**

```
Context: Our company is considering AI investments. We have:
- Identified threats: [TOP 3 THREATS FROM YOUR RED NOTES]
- Identified opportunities: [TOP 3 OPPORTUNITIES FROM YOUR GREEN NOTES]
- Current data maturity: [LEVEL FROM EXERCISE 1]

Your role: You are "AI Optimist" 

Make the STRONGEST case for investing aggressively in AI NOW, despite risks.
Address: Why wait = competitive death, data will improve with usage, first-mover advantages.

Keep responses to 3-4 sentences. Be persuasive but acknowledge real risks.

Present your opening argument now.
```

**Prompt for Claude (or same AI, second conversation):**

```
[SAME CONTEXT AS ABOVE]

Your role: You are "AI Pragmatist"

Make the STRONGEST case for proceeding cautiously—building data infrastructure 
and governance FIRST before major AI investments.
Address: Why rushing = waste, data readiness matters, ethical risks are real.

Keep responses to 3-4 sentences. Be persuasive but acknowledge opportunity costs.

Present your opening argument now.
```

**Activity:**
1. Submit both prompts, get opening arguments
2. Read both aloud
3. Copy "AI Optimist" argument, paste into Claude's conversation: "AI Pragmatist, respond to this argument:"
4. Copy "AI Pragmatist" response, paste into ChatGPT: "AI Optimist, counter this point:"
5. Let them debate 2-3 rounds

**Discussion questions:**
- Which AI made the stronger case? Why?
- Did the debate reveal considerations you hadn't thought of?
- How does this help clarify your actual decision?
- What's the synthesis? (Usually: "Invest in H1 quick wins NOW while building data for H2/H3")

## Phase 3: Human Synthesis (15 minutes)

**Group discussion (10 min):**

Compare human vs. AI contributions:

1. **What did AI identify that humans missed?**
   - Different perspectives?
   - Connections between ideas?
   - Threats from adjacent industries?

2. **What did humans identify that AI missed?**
   - Industry-specific nuances?
   - Political/cultural context?
   - Gut feelings about competitors?

3. **Which AI-generated insights are most valuable?**
   - Add these to your sticky notes (use different color to mark "AI-generated")

4. **How did AI change your strategic thinking?**
   - Did reverse prompting reveal hidden opportunities?
   - Did the debate clarify decision criteria?

**Key learning to discuss:**
- AI is **augmentation, not replacement**
- AI is great at: Breadth, alternatives, challenging assumptions, connecting dots
- AI needs: Context from you, judgment about relevance, human decision-making
- Best results: Human intuition + AI analysis

**Group presentations (5 min):**

Each table shares ONE of the following:
- Most surprising AI-generated threat
- Best opportunity revealed by reverse prompting
- Key insight from the AI debate

**Facilitator captures themes:**
- Common threads across tables
- Patterns in what AI added vs. what humans saw
- Evidence that AI is a powerful thought partner (when prompted well)

## Tips for Effective Prompting

**Do:**
- ✅ Provide context (industry, challenges, current state)
- ✅ Be specific in what you're asking for
- ✅ Ask AI to challenge your assumptions
- ✅ Use reverse prompting ("ask me questions")
- ✅ Compare multiple AI outputs

**Don't:**
- ❌ Accept first response—probe deeper with follow-ups
- ❌ Copy AI output blindly—evaluate relevance
- ❌ Expect AI to know your specific company context
- ❌ Forget that YOU make the strategic decision, not AI

**Advanced technique (if time):**
Ask AI to "think step by step" or "show your reasoning" for deeper insights.

## What If AI Isn't Available?

**Backup plan if no internet or AI access at tables:**

**Option A: Facilitator Demonstrates (Recommended)**
1. Project facilitator's laptop on screen
2. Use one table's sticky notes as live example
3. Show all three techniques in real-time:
   - Risk Deep-Dive with their threats
   - Reverse Prompting dialogue (facilitator answers)
   - AI Debate (prepare both sides)
4. All tables watch, discuss, learn

**Option B: Pre-Recorded Demo**
If internet is completely unavailable:
- Show pre-recorded video of AI interaction
- Pause at key moments for discussion
- Tables still do synthesis exercise

**Tables then continue with:**
- Brainstorm: "What questions WOULD AI ask us?"
- Role-play: "If I were AI, I'd say..."
- Discuss: "How would we improve this prompt?"
- Capture insights AI would likely surface

**The learning still happens:** 
- Watching good prompting teaches prompting
- Discussing AI's likely responses builds critical thinking
- The meta-lesson (AI as thought partner) still lands
- Participants leave knowing HOW to use AI on Monday

**Key message:** "You don't need AI in the room to learn AI thinking. But you'll want to try these techniques when you get back to your desk."

## Facilitation Notes

**Timing flexibility:**
- If tables are slow: Skip AI Debate, use that 10 min for deeper Risk/Reverse prompting
- If tables are fast: Add fourth round "AI as Devil's Advocate" (challenge your best idea)

**Energy management:**
- This is mentally demanding (both human + AI thinking)
- Keep it dynamic: switch modes every 10 min
- Encourage healthy skepticism: "Is this AI insight actually useful?"

**Technical issues:**
- If AI is slow: While waiting, discuss what you expect AI to say
- If AI gives poor response: Show how to improve the prompt (add context, be specific)
- If no internet: Facilitator demonstrates, becomes interactive discussion

**The goal:**
Participants leave thinking "I should use AI for strategic analysis" not "AI is magic" or "AI is useless."

```

```

```
Debate the decision. Each response: 3-4 sentences max.
```
