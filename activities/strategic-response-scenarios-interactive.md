# Strategic Response Scenarios - Interactive Voting Guide

**Purpose:** Anonymous, real-time voting on strategic AI decisions  
**Duration:** 15 minutes (5 min per scenario)  
**Method:** MS Forms + Discussion (or backup: show of hands)

---

## Setup Instructions for Facilitators

### Option 1: Microsoft Forms (Recommended)

**Create 3 separate forms (one per scenario):**

1. Go to https://forms.microsoft.com
2. Create "New Form" (no login required for responses)
3. **Settings**: Enable "Anonymous responses"
4. Add questions as below
5. Get QR code: Share → QR Code
6. Display QR code on slide during exercise

**Benefits:**
- ✅ Anonymous voting (people commit honestly)
- ✅ Real-time results visible to all
- ✅ No participant login required
- ✅ Shows distribution of choices
- ✅ Can add brief reasoning field

## FORM 1: Competitive Threat Response

### Form Title
**Strategic Decision: Competitive AI Response**

### Context (Display text)
Your main competitor launched an AI shopping assistant. Glowing reviews. Stock up 8%. Board wants your response by Friday.

### Question 1 (Multiple Choice - Required)
**What is your strategic response?**

- ○ **Option A**: Fast-Follow (6 months, $800K) - License existing platform, customize quickly
- ○ **Option B**: Differentiate (12 months, $1.5M) - Build proprietary AI with unique features
- ○ **Option C**: Wait & Watch (0 months, $0) - Monitor competitor, focus elsewhere

### Question 2 (Short Answer - Optional)
**Which framework influenced your decision most?**

Examples: AI Transformation Matrix, Three Horizons, Innovation Adoption Phase, Risk tolerance

_(Keep this short - 1-2 words max for real-time viewing)_

## FORM 2: Pilot Performance Decision

### Form Title
**Strategic Decision: Scale, Pivot, or Kill?**

### Context (Display text)
Your AI forecasting pilot is 9 months in. Results: 78% accuracy (target: 85%). Costs: $1.3M (budget: $1.1M). 3 of 10 stores don't trust the system.

### Question 1 (Multiple Choice - Required)
**What should you do?**

- ○ **Option A**: Scale as Planned - Deploy to 40 more stores ($2.5M additional)
- ○ **Option B**: Pivot the Approach - Fix issues for 6 months, then re-pilot ($400K additional)
- ○ **Option C**: Kill the Project - Stop losses, reallocate to higher-ROI initiative ($0 additional)

### Question 2 (Short Answer - Optional)
**What factor most influenced your decision?**

Examples: ROI trajectory, technical feasibility, market readiness, strategic fit

_(Keep short for real-time)_

### Form Title
**Strategic Decision: Innovation Timing**

### Context (Display text)
Multi-modal AI (text + image + video) shows breakthrough potential for retail. Early stage. Few enterprise implementations. Technology still evolving.

### Question 1 (Multiple Choice - Required)

## Exercise Flow (5 minutes per scenario)

### Before Exercise Starts (1 minute)
**Display slide with QR code:**
```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                                          │
│      STRATEGIC DECISION TIME             │
│                                          │
│      [QR CODE HERE]                      │
│                                          │
│   Scan to submit your decision          │
│   (Anonymous - no login required)       │
│                                          │
│   Or visit: [SHORT LINK]                │
│                                          │
└─────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Say:**
> "We're going to practice rapid strategic decisions. 
> 
> For each scenario, you'll vote anonymously using your phone or laptop. 
> Scan the QR code or visit the link on screen.
> 
> Then we'll see the results together and discuss WHY people chose different options.
> 
> No right answers—just strategic thinking under pressure."

### For Each Scenario:

**Step 1: Present Context (1 min)**
- Read scenario aloud
- Show options on slide
- Display QR code

**Step 2: Individual Decision (1.5 min)**
- "Take 90 seconds. Read options. Submit your choice."
- "Apply the frameworks from today: Transformation Matrix, Horizons, Adoption Phase"
- Show timer on screen

**Step 3: Show Results (30 sec)**
- Click "Responses" in MS Forms
- Display results graph on projector
- "Let's see what we decided..."

**Step 4: Discussion (2 min)**
- "Interesting! 40% chose A, 35% B, 25% C."
- "Who chose A? Why?" [30 sec]
- "Who chose B? What made B better than A?" [30 sec]
- "Who chose C? What did the rest of us miss?" [30 sec]
- "What framework did you use?" [30 sec]

### Key Facilitation Phrases:

**When results show split decision:**
> "Perfect split! This means it's genuinely hard. There's no obviously right answer. Let's hear the reasoning."

**When results show consensus:**
> "Strong consensus for Option B. But let's hear from the 20% who chose differently—what did they see?"

**When highlighting frameworks:**
> "I'm hearing Three Horizons thinking: 'This is H1, so we need fast response.'
> And I'm hearing Transformation Matrix: 'This is Enhance, not Transform.'"

**Connecting to afternoon:**
> "Notice how you're using the frameworks automatically now? This morning they were concepts. Now they're decision tools. That's the goal."

## Backup Plan: No Tech / Forms Not Working

### Low-Tech Alternative: Colored Cards

**Prepare before workshop:**
- Print 3 colored cards per participant
- Red = Option A
- Yellow = Option B  
- Green = Option C

**During exercise:**
1. Present scenario
2. "Hold up your card: Red for A, Yellow for B, Green for C"
3. Quickly count: "Looks like 40% Red, 30% Yellow, 30% Green"
4. Discuss as normal

**Or even simpler: Show of hands**
- "Who chose Option A? [count]"
- "Option B? [count]"
- "Option C? [count]"
- Works fine if forms fail!

## MS Forms Setup: Step-by-Step

### 1. Create Form
- Visit https://forms.microsoft.com
- Click "New Form"
- Title: "Strategic Decision: [Scenario Name]"

### 2. Add Context
- Click "+ Add new"
- Select "Text"
- Paste scenario context
- Format: Make key numbers **bold**

### 3. Add Question
- Click "+ Add new"
- Select "Choice"
- Enter question text
- Add 3 options (A, B, C with full descriptions)
- Toggle "Required" ON

### 4. Optional: Add Reasoning Field
- Click "+ Add new"
- Select "Text" (short answer)
- "What framework influenced your decision?"
- Toggle "Required" OFF

### 5. Configure Settings
- Click "..." menu → Settings
- **Anonymous responses**: ON
- **One response per person**: OFF (allow re-votes if they change mind)
- **Show results summary**: ON (so you can display)
- **Response receipts**: OFF

### 6. Get Links
- Click "Share"
- Click "QR Code" → Download PNG
- Copy short link (e.g., forms.office.com/r/abc123)
- Add both to your slides

### 7. Test Before Workshop
- Open form on phone
- Submit test response
- Confirm you can see results in real-time
- Practice displaying results on projector

## Facilitator Tips

### Before the Workshop:
✅ Create all 3 forms (15 minutes total setup)  
✅ Test each form on your phone  
✅ Download QR codes  
✅ Add QR codes to slides  
✅ Practice displaying live results  

### During Exercise:
✅ **Keep momentum** - 5 minutes per scenario is tight  
✅ **Show results immediately** - builds energy  
✅ **Focus on reasoning** - "Why did you choose that?"  
✅ **Connect to frameworks** - "I heard Three Horizons thinking..."  
✅ **No judgment** - All choices are valid with good reasoning  

### Common Issues:

**"Form is loading slowly"**
→ Have backup show-of-hands ready

**"QR code isn't scanning"**
→ Read short link aloud: "Visit forms.office.com/r/[CODE]"

**"Someone can't access form"**
→ "That's fine—think about your choice and share when we discuss"

**"Results are 100% one option"**
→ "Everyone agrees! But let's hear: What would make you choose differently?"

**"Results are perfectly split 33/33/33"**
→ "This is genuinely hard! No consensus. That's realistic—these are tough calls."

## Why This Works Better Than Show of Hands

**Anonymous voting solves:**
- ❌ Social pressure ("Everyone's choosing A, I should too")
- ❌ No commitment ("I'll just not raise my hand")
- ❌ Groupthink (first person influences others)
- ❌ Status effects (junior person follows senior person)

**Anonymous voting enables:**
- ✅ Honest choices
- ✅ Real commitment  
- ✅ Independent thinking
- ✅ Safe disagreement with majority
- ✅ Richer discussion ("Why did 30% choose differently?")

---

## Success Metrics

**You'll know this worked if:**

✅ **>80% participation** - Most people submit votes  
✅ **Split decisions** - Not everyone agrees (means it's genuinely hard)  
✅ **Framework language** - People cite Horizons, Matrix, Adoption Phase  
✅ **Lively debate** - "I chose B because..." "But didn't you consider...?"  
✅ **Aha moments** - "Oh, I didn't think about it from that angle"  
✅ **After exercise** - "That was useful—I'll use these frameworks on Monday"  

**Red flags:**
❌ Low participation (<50%) → Tech barrier too high, switch to show-of-hands  
❌ 90%+ consensus every time → Scenarios too easy, people not thinking critically  
❌ Silence during discussion → You're rushing, slow down, probe more  

---

## Time Management

**If running ahead of schedule:**
- Add 4th scenario from "Optional Extended Scenarios"
- Deeper discussion on each choice
- Cross-table debate: "Table 3 chose A, Table 5 chose C—discuss!"

**If running behind schedule:**
- Do 2 scenarios instead of 3
- Skip optional reasoning field in forms
- Show results but shorter discussion (1 min instead of 2)

**Critical:** Don't rush. Better to do 2 scenarios well than 3 rushed.
