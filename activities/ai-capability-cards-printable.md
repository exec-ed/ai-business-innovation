# AI Capability Cards - Printable Edition
**For Exercise 1: AI Tech Radar**

---

## Printing Instructions

**Format**: 4 cards per A4 page (landscape)  
**Pages**: 3 pages = 12 capability cards  
**Card size**: ~14.8cm × 10.5cm (half A4 landscape)

### How to Print:
1. Print pages 1-3 on **cardstock** (recommended) or regular paper
2. Use a paper cutter or scissors to cut along the dashed lines
3. Each page produces 4 cards
4. Optional: Laminate for durability

### Alternative Formats:
- **Digital use**: Display on projector or share as PDF
- **8 cards per page**: Reduce to 50% and print 2 per sheet for smaller cards

```
QUADRANT GUIDE:

OPTIMIZE (Process + Incremental)
  → Efficiency gains, cost reduction, automate routine work
  → Examples: RPA, Chatbots, Document Processing

  → Improve products/services, better customer experience
  → Examples: Recommendations, Dynamic Pricing, Gen AI

REVOLUTIONIZE (Process + Transformational)
  → Completely new ways of working, reinvent operations
  → Examples: Autonomous Agents, Full Automation

TRANSFORM (Strategic + Transformational)
  → New business models, new revenue streams
  → Examples: AI-as-a-Service, Platform Models
```

## Facilitator Tips

### Before the Session:
1. **Print 1 set per table** (4-6 participants per set)
2. **Use cardstock** (200-300gsm) for durability
3. **Consider laminating** if reusing across multiple sessions
4. **Prepare large matrix** on flipchart (1m × 1m minimum)

### During Exercise 1:
1. **Distribute cards** (5 min): Let participants browse all cards
2. **Individual reflection** (10 min): "Which capabilities does your org have/need?"
3. **Group mapping** (15 min): Place cards on matrix, discuss placement
4. **Gap analysis** (5 min): "What quadrants are empty?"

### Variations:
- **Digital projection**: Display cards on screen, use virtual sticky notes
- **Industry-specific**: Add blank cards for participants to create custom capabilities
- **Color coding**: Print quadrants in different colors (Optimize=Blue, Enhance=Green, etc.)

### Storage:
- Keep card sets in labeled envelopes
- One envelope per table/group
- Include matrix reference card in each envelope
