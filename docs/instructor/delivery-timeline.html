AI-Driven Business Innovation - One Day Masterclass

Course Overview

Duration: 9:00 AM - 4:30 PM (7.5 hours including breaks) Audience: Executives, strategists, innovation leaders, portfolio managers Style: Strategic simulation with AI-specific investment frameworks Goal: Learn to evaluate AI initiatives using BOTH traditional AND AI-specific criteria

What makes this AI-specific: - Data readiness assessment before investment - Continuous learning costs and plans - Accuracy vs. risk tolerance matching - Explainability requirements - Ethical risk and bias evaluation

⚠️ REVISED SCHEDULE: This version incorporates timing improvements from day-review-and-improvements.md

Daily Schedule & Timing (REVISED)

SESSION 1: AI Strategy Foundations (9:00 - 10:40 AM) - 100 min

9:00 - 9:10 AM: Welcome & Start Leadership Assessment (10 min)

  • Welcome (3 min): Course overview, objectives, what makes this AI-specific
  • Activity: Start AI Leadership Style Assessment (7 min)
    • Participants take on phones/laptops while facilitator sets up
    • 12 questions, ~5-7 minutes to complete
    • Results saved for end-of-day comparison
  • Materials: ai-leadership-style-assessment.html (link or QR code)
  • Note: This multitasks setup time with participant activity

9:10 - 9:25 AM: Assessment Results & Roundtable (15 min)

  • Activity: Share leadership styles and discuss
  • Format: Quick overview of 4 styles (5 min), then roundtable (10 min, 30 sec each)
  • Styles: Strategic Visionary, ROI Pragmatist, Bold Experimenter, Capability Builder
  • Outcome: Understand diverse perspectives in room, create self-awareness
  • Key question: “What makes AI investment different from traditional IT?”
  • Facilitator note: This replaces generic introductions with meaningful self-assessment

9:25 - 9:55 AM: What Makes AI Different? (30 min)

  • NEW CONTENT: AI-specific investment considerations
  • Format: Mini-lecture with examples
  • Content:
    • 5 ways AI differs: Data dependency, continuous learning, probabilistic, black box, ethical risks
    • Traditional frameworks vs. AI-specific criteria
    • Preview of AI Investment Checklist
  • Materials: Why AI Innovation is Different (from pre-reading)
  • Learning: AI requires ADDITIONAL evaluation criteria beyond traditional ROI

9:55 - 10:40 AM: Exercise 1 - AI Tech Radar with Data Readiness (45 min)

  • Format: Group assessment of AI capabilities
  • Activity:
    • Map your organisation on AI Transformation Matrix (20 min)
    • NEW: Assess data readiness for each initiative (15 min)
    • NEW: Flag ethical risks (10 min)
  • Materials: AI capability cards (printable version), tech radar template, data readiness scorecard
  • Learning: Where is your organisation today? Is your data ready for AI?

BREAK (10:40 - 11:00 AM) - 20 min

SESSION 2: Strategic Frameworks (11:00 AM - 12:30 PM) - 90 min

11:00 - 11:20 AM: Framework 1 - Data Value Pyramid & Three Horizons (20 min)

  • REVISED: Compressed from 30 min to 20 min (focus on key concepts)
  • Content:
    • Data maturity progression (Level 1-5: Collection → Autonomous)
    • Portfolio balancing (Horizon 1: optimize 70%, Horizon 2: emerging 20%, Horizon 3: transform 10%)
    • NEW: Match AI ambition to data maturity
  • Format: Framework explanation with RetailFlow examples
  • Learning: Can’t do Level 5 AI with Level 2 data. Build foundations first.
  • Facilitator note: Focus on core concepts, skip deep technical details

11:20 - 12:30 PM: Exercise 2 - AI-Assisted Strategic Analysis (70 min)

  • REVISED: Expanded from 60 min to 70 min (buffer for first-time AI users + tech issues)

  • Content:

    • Data maturity progression (Level 1-5: Collection → Autonomous)
    • Portfolio balancing (Horizon 1: optimize 70%, Horizon 2: emerging 20%, Horizon 3: transform 10%)
    • NEW: Match AI ambition to data maturity

  • Format: Framework explanation with RetailFlow examples

  • Learning: Can’t do Level 5 AI with Level 2 data. Build foundations first.

  • Format: Human brainstorming + AI augmentation + synthesis

  • NEW: Hands-on use of AI tools (Google Gemini/ChatGPT/Claude) for strategic thinking

  • Activity:

    • Phase 1: Human brainstorm threats & opportunities (15 min)
    • Phase 2: AI augmentation (40 min)
      • Risk Deep-Dive: AI identifies what you’re missing (12 min)
      • Reverse Prompting: AI asks YOU questions to reveal opportunities (13 min)
      • AI Debate: Two AIs argue different perspectives (10 min) - OPTIONAL if time
      • Buffer for tech issues (5 min)
    • Phase 3: Human synthesis - Compare human vs. AI insights (15 min)

  • Technology (BYOD): Tables self-organize with participant devices

    • Recommended: Google Gemini (free, no login) or ChatGPT/Claude
    • Each table designates “AI Lead” to coordinate
    • Manual typing of sticky notes (no photo upload)

  • Materials: Strategic Prompting Guide, Three Horizons grid, sticky notes (multiple colors)

  • Learning:

    • AI as strategic thought partner (augmentation, not replacement)
    • Prompt engineering for business insights
    • AI strengths (breadth, alternatives) and limitations (needs context)
    • Deeper goal: Experience AI value so they naturally use it this afternoon

  • Facilitator note: This is first hands-on AI use—expect tech issues. Have backup demo ready.

LUNCH BREAK (12:30 - 1:15 PM) - 45 min

SESSION 3: AI Investment Framework & Dragon’s Den Setup (1:15 - 2:30 PM) - 75 min

1:15 - 1:30 PM: Exercise 4 - Strategic Response Scenarios (15 min)

  • REVISED: Moved from end of day to post-lunch energizer
  • Format: Rapid strategic decisions with anonymous voting
  • Technology: MS Forms with QR codes (or show-of-hands backup)
  • Activity:
    • Present 3 scenarios (competitive threat, pilot performance, emerging tech)
    • 90 seconds to vote per scenario
    • Show live results, discuss reasoning (3-4 min per scenario)
  • Materials: strategic-response-scenarios-interactive.md, MS Forms with QR codes
  • Learning: Apply morning frameworks (Transformation Matrix, Horizons, Adoption Phase) to rapid decisions
  • Why here: Energizes post-lunch slump, validates morning learning, quick wins before heavy afternoon
  • Facilitator note: Keep pace tight (5 min per scenario max)

1:30 - 2:00 PM: Framework 2 - AI Investment Model (30 min)

  • REVISED: Compressed from original position, still covers all content
  • Content: Traditional ROI PLUS AI-specific criteria
  • Format: Framework walkthrough with RetailFlow example
  • Traditional criteria:
    • Value categories (cost reduction, revenue growth, risk reduction, strategic positioning)
    • Three Horizons fit
    • Portfolio balance
  • NEW AI-specific criteria:
    • Data readiness score (0-10, need 7+)
    • Continuous learning plan & costs (30-50% annually)
    • Accuracy requirements vs. AI capability
    • Explainability needs
    • Ethical risk assessment
  • Materials: AI Investment Model template, AI Investment Checklist
  • Learning: Highest ROI ≠ best AI investment. Must pass BOTH traditional AND AI criteria.

2:00 - 2:30 PM: Dragon’s Den Setup & Team Preparation (30 min)

  • REVISED: Compressed from 45 min to 30 min (still sufficient for prep)
  • Activity: Form teams and begin pitch preparation
  • Setup (10 min):
    • Divide into “Investment Committee” (4-5 people) and “Project Teams” (3 teams of 3-4 people)
    • Assign scenarios: Chatbot, Dynamic Pricing, Inventory Optimization (Fraud Detection is pre-prepared)
    • Explain evaluation criteria (traditional + AI-specific)
  • Team Preparation (35 min):
    • Project teams: Prepare 7-minute pitch using AI Investment Checklist
    • Investment Committee: Review AI Investment Checklist and scoring rubric
    • NEW: Teams must address all 5 AI-specific criteria (data, learning, accuracy, explainability, ethics)
    • Organic AI use: AI tools (Gemini/ChatGPT/Claude) available if teams choose to use them
      • Example: “Let’s ask AI what risks we’re missing in our fraud detection pitch”
      • Don’t mandate—measure of success is they CHOOSE to use it after morning experience
  • Materials: Investment pitch scenarios (with AI-specific sections), AI Investment Checklist, investment-calculator.html
  • Facilitator note: 30 min is tight but achievable—teams are energized and focused

BREAK (2:30 - 2:45 PM) - 15 min

  • REVISED: Shortened from 30 min to 15 min to maintain momentum
  • Why: Teams just finished prep and are energized—long break kills energy
  • Quick bio break + coffee: Enough for refresh, keeps momentum high

SESSION 4: Dragon’s Den & Strategic Synthesis (2:45 - 4:30 PM) - 105 min

2:45 - 3:55 PM: Exercise 3 - AI Investment Dragon’s Den (70 min)

  • REVISED: Starts at 2:45 instead of 3:00 (earlier break maintains momentum)
  • Format: Investment pitch simulation
  • Structure:
    • Pitch 1: Chatbot (15 min total)
      • 7 min pitch (traditional ROI + AI-specific criteria)
      • 5 min Q&A from committee (probe on data, bias, explainability)
      • 3 min committee private scoring
    • Pitch 2: Dynamic Pricing (15 min total)
      • 7 min pitch
      • 5 min Q&A (ethical concerns about discrimination?)
      • 3 min committee scoring
    • Pitch 3: Inventory Optimization (15 min total)
      • 7 min pitch
      • 5 min Q&A (data readiness? change management?)
      • 3 min committee scoring
    • Pitch 4: Fraud Detection (10 min)
      • Pre-prepared pitch presented by facilitator (7 min)
      • Committee Q&A (3 min)
    • Committee Deliberation (10 min)
      • $2M budget to allocate
      • Must consider traditional ROI + AI-specific risks
      • Some projects may not be fundable due to AI-specific red flags
    • Investment Decision Announcement (5 min)
      • Committee announces allocation with rationale
      • Must explain which AI-specific criteria drove decisions
  • NEW Learning:
    • Dynamic pricing has HIGHEST ROI but exceeds budget + high ethical risk
    • Inventory optimization strong but exceeds budget cap slightly
    • Fraud detection needs 6-month data prep first
    • Chatbot is “safest” but lowest ROI
    • The dilemma: Highest ROI isn’t always the best AI investment

3:55 - 4:10 PM: Debrief & Key Insights (15 min)

  • REVISED: Starts at 3:55 instead of 4:10 (earlier Dragon’s Den finish)
  • Facilitated discussion:
    • How did AI-specific criteria change your evaluation?
    • What surprised you about the “hidden costs” (data, bias testing, explainability)?
    • Which project would you have funded without AI-specific analysis?
    • How will you apply this to your real AI investments?
  • Key takeaways:
    • AI requires different evaluation than traditional IT
    • Data readiness gates everything
    • Ethical risks are real and expensive to mitigate
    • Ongoing costs are 30-50% higher than traditional software

4:10 - 4:25 PM: Personal Action Planning (15 min)

  • REVISED: Expanded from 5 min to 15 min for meaningful reflection
  • Activity:
    • Individual reflection (5 min): Use personal-action-plan-template.md
      • What AI initiative will you evaluate differently now?
      • What’s your organization’s data readiness score (0-10)?
      • Which AI-specific criterion is your biggest concern?
      • What will you do Monday morning?
    • Pair share (5 min): Turn to neighbor, share your top action
    • Group share (5 min): 2-3 volunteers share with full group
  • Materials: Personal action plan template
  • Facilitator note: This is closure—don’t rush it. Let people process the day.

4:25 - 4:30 PM: Closing & Optional Assessment Retake (5 min)

  • NEW: Optional leadership assessment retake for before/after comparison
  • Activity:
    • Retake ai-leadership-style-assessment.html (shows comparison with morning results)
    • See how leadership style evolved during the day
    • Creates powerful “learning impact” moment
  • Closing remarks:
    • Thank you for engagement
    • Contact info for questions
    • Link to digital materials
  • Note: Assessment retake is optional but powerful—shows workshop impact

Key Frameworks Taught

  1. AI Transformation Matrix - Classify initiatives (process vs strategic, incremental vs transformational)
  2. Data Value Pyramid - 5-level data maturity (gate AI ambition to data capability)
  3. Three Horizons Model - Balance portfolio across timeframes (70/20/10 rule)
  4. AI Investment Model - Traditional ROI + 5 AI-specific criteria
  5. AI Investment Checklist - 10 questions every AI project must answer

NEW: AI-Specific Evaluation Criteria

Every AI investment must satisfy BOTH:

A. Traditional Investment Criteria

  • Strategic fit (Three Horizons, Transformation Matrix)
  • Financial ROI (cost reduction, revenue growth, risk, strategic)
  • Implementation feasibility

B. AI-Specific Investment Criteria (NEW)

  1. Data Readiness (0-10 score, need 7+)
  2. Continuous Learning Plan (30-50% ongoing costs)
  3. Accuracy vs. Risk Tolerance (match requirements to capability)
  4. Explainability Requirements (regulatory, operational, customer)
  5. Ethical Risk Assessment (bias testing, diverse teams, monitoring)

Decision rule: Must pass BOTH sets of criteria
