Five Strategic Frameworks

Proven frameworks for making strategic AI investment decisions.

1

AI Transformation Matrix

Classify AI initiatives across two dimensions: Process vs. Strategic, and Incremental vs. Transformational.

TRANSFORMATIONAL │ REVOLUTIONIZE │ TRANSFORM (New Processes)│ (New Business Models) ───────────────┼─────────────── OPTIMIZE │ ENHANCE (Efficiency) │ (Better Products) │ INCREMENTAL
Strategic Application: Balance your AI portfolio across all four quadrants. Most organisations over-invest in "Optimize" - identify strategic gaps.
2

Data Value Pyramid

Five levels of data maturity from basic collection to autonomous operations. You can't jump to Level 5 AI without Level 3+ data foundations.

┌─────────────┐ │ AUTONOMOUS │ Level 5 ├─────────────┤ │ PRESCRIPTIVE│ Level 4 ├─────────────┤ │ PREDICTIVE │ Level 3 ├─────────────┤ │ DIAGNOSTIC │ Level 2 ├─────────────┤ │ DESCRIPTIVE │ Level 1 └─────────────┘
Strategic Application: Assess your data maturity before investing in advanced AI. Match AI initiatives to your current data capabilities.
3

Three Horizons Model

Balance your AI investment portfolio across three time horizons with different risk/return profiles.

Horizon 1: Extend & Defend Core (60-70%) • 0-12 months payback • Low risk, proven ROI • Optimize current operations Horizon 2: Build Emerging Capabilities (20-30%) • 1-3 years payback • Medium risk, strategic value • Adjacent opportunities Horizon 3: Create Transformational Options (10-15%) • 3-5+ years payback • High risk, breakthrough potential • Future business models
Strategic Application: Evaluate whether your AI portfolio is balanced. All H1 = no future. All H3 = no present. Balance creates sustainable innovation.
4

AI Investment Model

Calculate ROI across four value categories to quantify business impact systematically.

1. Cost Reduction • Labour savings • Process efficiency • Resource optimization 2. Revenue Growth • New sales • Retention improvement • Pricing power 3. Risk Reduction • Fraud prevention • Compliance • Error reduction 4. Strategic Positioning • Competitive advantage • Capability building • Market leadership
Strategic Application: Move beyond "AI for AI's sake." Quantify value rigorously. Compare initiatives on consistent criteria.
5

Innovation Adoption Framework

Five-phase journey from knowledge building to comprehensive transformation. Respect the journey - don't skip phases.

Phase 1: Knowledge Building (3-6 mo) Phase 2: Pilot Experimentation (6-12 mo) Phase 3: Selective Deployment (12-18 mo) Phase 4: Scaling Operations (18-36 mo) Phase 5: Comprehensive Transformation (36+ mo) At each phase: Scale, Pivot, or Kill
Strategic Application: Understand where each initiative is in the journey. Use Scale/Pivot/Kill criteria honestly at each phase gate.
