Strategic Exercises

Four exercises to practice strategic AI decision-making using real investment scenarios.

Exercise 1: AI Tech Radar

Portfolio mapping

45 min

Map your organisation's AI capabilities across the Transformation Matrix. Use 12 AI capability cards to assess where you're over-invested, under-invested, and identify strategic gaps.

Key Learning: Most organisations cluster in "Optimize" quadrant (60-80%). Strategic leaders balance across all four quadrants intentionally.

Exercise 2: Threat & Opportunity Mapping

Competitive strategy

60 min

Identify competitive threats from AI (how could competitors disrupt you?) and strategic opportunities (how could you use AI for advantage?). Position opportunities across Three Horizons.

Key Learning: AI is both threat and opportunity. Most teams identify more H1 opportunities than H2/H3 - pushing strategic thinking reveals transformational options.

Exercise 3: Dragon's Den Investment Simulation

Investment pitch & evaluation

90 min

⭐ THE CORE LEARNING ACTIVITY ⭐

This simulation is where strategic frameworks meet real investment decisions

The Challenge: RetailFlow has $2M AI budget. Four competing initiatives. Investment Committee must allocate budget strategically.

Four AI Initiatives:

  • Customer Service Chatbot ($450K, 92% ROI, H1 Optimize)
  • Dynamic Pricing ($850K, 253% ROI, H2 Enhance)
  • Inventory Optimization ($1.1M, 264% ROI, H1-H2 Revolutionize)
  • Fraud Detection ($650K, 315% ROI, H1 Optimize)

Project Teams prepare 10-minute pitches using Investment Model framework. Committee evaluates, questions, deliberates, and announces investment decisions with strategic rationale.

Key Learning: Highest ROI doesn't always win. Strategic fit, portfolio balance, capability building, and timing all matter. Practice making tough investment calls with limited budget.

Exercise 4: Strategic Response Scenarios

Quick decision practice

15 min

Three rapid strategic decisions: (1) Competitive threat response - fast-follow, differentiate, or wait? (2) Pilot performance - scale, pivot, or kill? (3) Emerging tech - pioneer, fast-follow, or wait?

Key Learning: Strategic agility = frameworks + judgment. Practice using frameworks for faster, better decisions under time pressure.
