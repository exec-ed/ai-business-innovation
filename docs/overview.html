Course Overview

This is a strategic leadership masterclass on evaluating, prioritising, and investing in AI initiatives. Using the Dragon's Den investment simulation and proven frameworks, you'll practice making real portfolio decisions. The focus is on investment decision-making and organisational readiness — not technical implementation or hands-on coding.

🎯 Who Should Attend

  • C-suite executives and senior leaders
  • Innovation officers and strategists
  • Portfolio managers
  • Business unit leaders
  • Investment committee members

📚 What You'll Learn

  • 5 strategic frameworks for AI investment
  • Portfolio balancing across time horizons
  • ROI calculation for AI initiatives
  • Strategic positioning with AI
  • Investment decision-making

🚀 Learning Approach

  • Dragon's Den investment simulation
  • Strategic exercises (50% of time)
  • RetailFlow investment decisions
  • Peer evaluation and debate
  • Personal action planning
