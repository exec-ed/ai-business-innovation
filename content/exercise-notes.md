# Business Innovation Course Exercises

## Exercise 1: AI Tech Radar (45 mins)

### Setup
- 15-20 AI capability cards (computer vision, NLP, predictive analytics, etc.)
- Each card includes: maturity level, cost range, implementation time, industry applications
- 2x2 matrix: "Strategic Value vs. Implementation Readiness"

### Process
1. Groups analyze AI capability cards
2. Plot technologies on strategic matrix
3. Discuss industry-specific priorities
4. Debrief on strategic vs. table stakes AI

### Materials Needed
- AI capability cards (print/digital)
- Strategic matrix template
- Industry-specific scenario briefs

---

## Exercise 2: Threat & Opportunity Mapping (45 mins)

### Setup
- CloudCore virtual company case study
- Competitor intelligence briefings
- Market analysis frameworks

### Process
1. Analyze current competitive landscape
2. Identify AI-powered disruption threats
3. Map defensive and offensive AI opportunities
4. Develop strategic positioning recommendations

### Key Questions
- "If we do nothing, what AI-powered competitor could disrupt us?"
- "What AI investments create defensible competitive advantage?"

---

## Exercise 3: AI Investment Dragon's Den (90 mins)

### Setup
- Groups receive $10M budget
- 5 AI project proposals with incomplete business cases
- ROI modeling templates
- Risk assessment frameworks

### Project Proposals
1. Predictive customer churn (proven tech, moderate ROI)
2. AI-powered product recommendation (competitive parity)
3. Generative AI for customer service (high risk, transformational)
4. Computer vision for quality control (industry-specific)
5. AI-driven pricing optimization (quick win)

### Process
1. Complete financial modeling for each option
2. Decide which projects to fund and why
3. Build 3-year implementation roadmap
4. Present to "board" (peer groups + facilitator)

---

## Exercise 4: Strategic Simulation - Market Response (60 mins)

### Setup
- Groups have made investment decisions
- Market event cards prepared
- Real-time adaptation framework

### Market Events
- "Competitor launched AI product similar to your investment"
- "New regulation affects your data strategy"
- "Breakthrough technology emerged - do you pivot?"

### Process
1. Introduce market events sequentially
2. Groups must adapt strategy in real-time
3. Document decision-making rationale
4. Debrief on strategic agility principles

---

## Exercise 5: Strategy Synthesis (30 mins)

### Individual Reflection
- "What's one AI investment you'll champion at your organization?"
- Specific action planning
- Risk assessment and mitigation strategies

### Group Sharing
- Peer feedback on proposals
- Cross-pollination of ideas
- Commitment to next steps
