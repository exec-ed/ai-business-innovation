## Pre-Course Preparation

### Self-Assessment (Complete before arrival)
1. **Current AI Maturity**
   - Where is your organization on the AI adoption curve?
   - What AI initiatives are currently underway?
   - What barriers exist to AI adoption?

2. **Strategic Priorities**
   - What are your top 3 business challenges AI could address?
   - What competitive threats keep you awake at night?
   - Where could AI create sustainable advantage?

3. **Resource Assessment**
   - What's your current AI investment capacity?
   - What skills and capabilities exist internally?
   - What partnerships or acquisitions are you considering?

## Case Study Preview

### CloudCore Virtual Company
You'll be analyzing CloudCore, a mid-sized technology services company facing:

- **Market Pressure**: AI-powered competitors entering their space
- **Internal Capability Gaps**: Limited AI expertise and infrastructure
- **Executive Mandate**: CEO demanding AI transformation strategy
- **Resource Constraints**: $10M annual AI investment budget

### Pre-Case Questions
1. What industries does CloudCore operate in?
2. Who are their main competitors?
3. What are their core business processes?
4. Where could AI create the most value?

## Technology Landscape Overview

### AI Capability Areas to Understand
1. **Predictive Analytics** - Forecasting and optimization
2. **Natural Language Processing** - Text and speech understanding
3. **Computer Vision** - Image and video analysis
4. **Generative AI** - Content creation and synthesis
5. **Robotics & Automation** - Physical process automation

### Maturity Levels
- **Emerging** - Experimental, high risk, high potential
- **Growth** - Proven in limited applications, scaling challenges
- **Mature** - Widely adopted, competitive parity required
- **Commodity** - Table stakes, cost of doing business

## Strategic Frameworks Preview

### Investment Decision Matrix
- **Strategic Value vs. Implementation Readiness**
- **Offensive vs. Defensive AI Applications**
- **Short-term Wins vs. Long-term Transformation**

### Risk Assessment Categories
- **Technology Risk** - Capability and feasibility
- **Market Risk** - Adoption and competitive response
- **Operational Risk** - Implementation and integration
- **Ethical Risk** - Bias, privacy, and regulatory compliance

## Discussion Starters

### For Group Discussion
1. What's the difference between "AI for efficiency" vs. "AI for growth"?
2. When should AI be a core competency vs. a utility service?
3. How do you balance quick wins with transformational projects?
4. What role should ethics play in AI investment decisions?

### Individual Reflection
1. What's your personal AI investment philosophy?
2. What biases might influence your AI investment decisions?
3. How do you measure success in AI initiatives?
4. What's your biggest fear about AI transformation?
