# AI to Drive Business Innovation Masterclass

## Overview
Strategic decision-making program for executives, strategists, and portfolio managers making AI investment decisions.

## Target Audience
- Executives and C-suite leaders
- Strategic planners and portfolio managers  
- Innovation officers and business unit leaders
- Investment committee members

## Learning Objectives
- Evaluate AI opportunities using strategic frameworks
- Build AI investment portfolios and roadmaps
- Navigate competitive dynamics in AI markets
- Lead organizational AI transformation

## Course Structure

### Morning Session: AI Opportunity Scanning & Evaluation
- **AI Tech Radar**: Capability assessment and prioritization
- **Threat & Opportunity Mapping**: Competitive intelligence analysis
- **Strategic Matrix Development**: Value vs. readiness positioning

### Afternoon Session: Portfolio Decision-Making
- **AI Investment Dragon's Den**: Project funding simulation
- **Strategic Market Response**: Real-time adaptation challenges
- **Strategy Synthesis**: Personal application planning

## Key Exercises
1. **AI Tech Radar** (45 mins) - Technology capability mapping
2. **Threat & Opportunity Mapping** (45 mins) - Competitive analysis
3. **AI Investment Dragon's Den** (90 mins) - Portfolio simulation
4. **Strategic Simulation** (60 mins) - Market response scenarios

## Materials
- Pre-readings and case studies
- AI capability cards and assessment frameworks
- Investment proposal templates
- Strategic planning worksheets
