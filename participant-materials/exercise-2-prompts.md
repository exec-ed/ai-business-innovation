## TECHNIQUE 1: RISK DEEP-DIVE
*Use AI to find threats and blind spots you might have overlooked*

### Basic Template:
```
I'm planning to [describe AI initiative]. What are 3-5 risks I might have overlooked?
```

### Example Prompts:

**Chatbot Risk Analysis:**
```
I'm planning to deploy AI chatbots for customer service in retail.
What are 3 risks I might have overlooked?
```

**Dynamic Pricing Risks:**
```
We're implementing dynamic pricing AI in our retail stores.
What could go wrong that I haven't considered?
```

**Using RetailFlow Context:**
```
[Upload RetailFlow Company Overview first]

RetailFlow is considering AI inventory optimization ($1.1M investment).
What are 5 risks specific to a mid-sized retailer like RetailFlow that
might not be obvious from the ROI projections?
```

## TECHNIQUE 2: REVERSE PROMPTING
*Let AI interview YOU to reveal gaps in your thinking*

### Basic Template:
```
I'm evaluating [AI initiative]. Ask me [5-10] critical questions I should answer before investing.
```

### Example Prompts:

**General AI Investment Questions:**
```
I need to evaluate an AI project for my organization.
Ask me 5 critical questions I should answer before investing.
```

**Specific Initiative Questions:**

## TECHNIQUE 3: AI DEBATE
*Create a strategic debate to see multiple perspectives*

### VERSION A: Single AI, Both Sides (Easiest)

**Basic Template:**
```
Facilitate a strategic debate about [decision]:

**Role 1 (PRO):** Argues for [option A]. Present 3 compelling arguments.
**Role 2 (CON):** Argues for [option B]. Present 3 compelling arguments.

After presenting both sides, have each side rebut the other's strongest argument.
```

**Example: Investment Decision**
```
Facilitate a strategic debate about RetailFlow's AI investment decision:

**Chief Innovation Officer (PRO):**
Argues we should invest $850K in AI dynamic pricing NOW.
Present 3 compelling arguments for immediate action.

**Chief Financial Officer (CON):**
Argues we should wait 18 months before investing.
Present 3 compelling arguments for delaying investment.

After presenting both sides, have each executive rebut the other's strongest argument.
```

**Example: Scale vs Kill Decision**
```
Debate: Should we scale our AI chatbot pilot or kill the project?

**VP Customer Service (SCALE):**
The pilot showed 65% automation. Argue for full rollout. 3 reasons.

**CFO (KILL):**
ROI is only 119% over 3 years. Argue for killing it. 3 reasons.

Each side rebuts the other's top concern.
```

### VERSION B: Two AIs Cross-Critique (Most Interactive)

**Requires two different AI tools (e.g., ChatGPT + Claude)**

**Step 1 - Ask BOTH AIs the same question:**
```
Should RetailFlow invest $1.3M in AI dynamic pricing now, or wait 18 months?
Present your recommendation with 3 supporting arguments.
```

**Step 2 - Copy AI #1's answer, paste into AI #2:**
```
Another AI strategist gave this recommendation:

[PASTE AI #1's FULL ANSWER HERE]

Critique this recommendation. What are the 3 weakest points in their argument?
```

**Step 3 (Optional) - Copy AI #2's critique, paste into AI #1:**
```
Another strategist critiqued my recommendation with these points:

[PASTE AI #2's CRITIQUE HERE]

Respond to these criticisms. Where is their critique wrong or incomplete?
```

### VERSION C: Two AIs, Assigned Sides

**AI #1 (ChatGPT) - PRO side:**
```
Argue FOR investing $1.3M in AI dynamic pricing NOW.
Give 3 strong arguments supporting immediate investment.
```

**AI #2 (Claude) - CON side:**
```
Argue AGAINST investing in AI dynamic pricing now. Recommend waiting 18 months.
Give 3 strong arguments for delaying investment.
```

**Then Cross-Critique (paste AI #1's answer into AI #2):**
```
Another strategist argued FOR immediate investment:

[PASTE AI #1's ANSWER]

Critique their arguments. What are the 3 weakest points?
```

## TIPS FOR EFFECTIVE PROMPTING

### 1. **Provide Context**
Upload the RetailFlow Company Overview or paste key facts:
- Company size: 500 employees, $500M revenue, 75 stores
- Budget constraint: $2M total for AI investments
- Strategic priorities: Growth + operational efficiency

### 2. **Be Specific**
❌ "What about AI?"
✅ "What are 3 risks of deploying AI chatbots for customer service in retail?"

### 3. **Ask for Structure**
- "Give me 3-5 examples"
- "Present this as a pros/cons table"
- "Rank these by priority"

### 4. **Iterate**
- Start with a broad question
- Follow up: "Elaborate on point #2"
- Challenge: "What if our budget is cut in half?"

### 5. **Use Multiple AIs**
- Different AIs have different "personalities"
- ChatGPT: Balanced, comprehensive
- Claude: Detail-oriented, cautious
- Gemini: Creative, sometimes contrarian

   - Discuss: When is AI helpful? When is human judgment critical?

## KEY LEARNING

**AI as Augmentation (not replacement):**
- ✅ AI finds blind spots you missed
- ✅ AI generates alternatives quickly
- ✅ AI challenges your assumptions

**But:**
- ❌ AI doesn't understand your organization's culture
- ❌ AI lacks context about stakeholder politics
- ❌ AI can't make the final decision (you must)

**Best practice:** Human expertise + AI analysis = Better strategic decisions
