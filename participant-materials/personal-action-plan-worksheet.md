## Part 1: Your AI Maturity Assessment (10 minutes)

### A. Where are you on the Data Value Pyramid?

Check ONE level that best describes your current state:

- [ ] **Level 1: Data Collection** – Raw data from operations, not yet integrated
  - _Example: Recording transactions, but data sits in siloed systems_

- [ ] **Level 2: Data Integration** – Connecting data sources, basic reporting
  - _Example: Sales + inventory + customer data in one place, can create dashboards_

- [ ] **Level 3: Descriptive Analytics** – Understanding what happened, finding patterns
  - _Example: Analysing trends, segmenting customers, identifying demand patterns_
