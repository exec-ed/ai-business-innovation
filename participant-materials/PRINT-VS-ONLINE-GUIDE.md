## Detailed Recommendations

### 📄 MUST PRINT (Core Course Materials)

#### 1. **frameworks-reference-sheet.md** ⭐ ESSENTIAL
**Why print:**
- Constant reference throughout the day
- Participants flip back and forth during exercises
- Quick lookup for all 5 frameworks
- Small enough to keep on desk (2-3 pages)

**Format:** Letter/A4, stapled, 2-3 pages
**Quantity:** 1 per participant + 3 spares
**When distributed:** At seat, before 9:00 AM start

---

#### 2. **ai-investment-checklist.md** ⭐ ESSENTIAL
**Why print:**
- Used heavily during Dragon's Den (3:00-4:10 PM)
- Investment Committee needs to score proposals
- Participants mark it up with notes
- Core evaluation tool for the course

**Format:** Letter/A4, stapled, 2-4 pages
**Quantity:** 1 per participant + 5 extras for Investment Committee
**When distributed:** At seat, before 9:00 AM (or during AI Investment Model session at 1:15 PM)

**Alternative:** Include in participant packet with frameworks reference sheet

---

#### 3. **how-to-use-retailflow.md** ⭐ ESSENTIAL
**Why print:**
- Distributed WITH RetailFlow case (10:00 AM, Dragon's Den setup)
- Sets expectations for case study approach
- Participants reference during preparation
- Helps them connect to their own organizations

**Format:** Letter/A4, 2-sided single sheet (fits on 1-2 pages)
**Quantity:** 1 per participant + 3 spares
**When distributed:** WITH RetailFlow case at Dragon's Den setup (10:00 AM or when Exercise 3 begins)

**Pro tip:** Staple or paper-clip this TO the RetailFlow case so they get both together

---

#### 4. **retailflow-company-overview.txt** ⭐ ESSENTIAL
**Why print:**
- Core case study document for Dragon's Den
- Teams reference constantly during pitch prep and evaluation
- Needs to be marked up, annotated
- Investment Committee checks financial details repeatedly

**Format:** Letter/A4, stapled, ~8 pages
**Quantity:** 1 per participant + 5 spares
**When distributed:** Dragon's Den setup (10:00 AM or when Exercise 3 begins)

**Pro tip:** Print with wide margins for note-taking

---

#### 5. **personal-action-plan-worksheet.md** ⭐ ESSENTIAL
**Why print:**
- Take-home exercise (45-60 minutes post-course)
- Participants write on it
- Physical worksheet feels more actionable than digital
- Can complete without a device

**Format:** Letter/A4, stapled, ~10 pages
**Quantity:** 1 per participant + 3 spares
**When distributed:** At seat, before 9:00 AM (in take-home packet) OR at end of day (4:25 PM)

**Alternative:** Include in take-home materials packet given at end of day

---

### ⚠️ OPTIONAL PRINT (Context-Dependent)

#### 6. **strategic-prompting-guide.md**
**Why it might be printed:**
- Used during Exercise 2 (AI-Assisted Strategic Analysis)
- Participants reference AI prompting techniques
- Helpful for those unfamiliar with AI tools

**Why it might NOT be printed:**
- Most executives already use ChatGPT/Claude
- Can be displayed on their laptops during exercise
- Less critical than other materials

**Recommendation:**
- **Print 10 copies** and have available if requested
- Most participants won't need it
- Have it available online

**Format:** Letter/A4, stapled, 5-8 pages
**When distributed:** During Exercise 2 setup (11:30 AM) - only if requested

#### 7. **ai-investment-checklist-interactive.html**

#### 10. **preparing-for-the-masterclass.txt**
**Why online only:**
- Reference document, not course material
- Content now in pre-course email
- Participants don't need it during course

**How to share:**
- Available on course website under "Course Info"
- Optional reference for those who want it

## Recommended Printing Strategy

### Option 1: Individual Packets (Recommended for <20 participants)

**Participant Packet (distributed at seat, 9:00 AM):**
1. Frameworks Reference Sheet (2-3 pages)
2. AI Investment Checklist (2-4 pages)
3. Personal Action Plan Worksheet (10 pages)

**Total:** ~15 pages per participant, stapled in sections

**Dragon's Den Packet (distributed 10:00 AM):**
1. How to Use RetailFlow (1-2 pages)
2. RetailFlow Company Overview (8 pages)

**Total:** ~10 pages per participant, stapled together

**Total per participant:** ~25 pages

---

### Option 2: Shared Materials (Recommended for >20 participants or budget-conscious)

**Individual materials (1 per participant):**
- Frameworks Reference Sheet (2-3 pages) - MUST have
- Personal Action Plan Worksheet (10 pages) - MUST have

**Shared/table materials:**
- AI Investment Checklist - 2 copies per table of 4-6
- RetailFlow Company Overview - 2 copies per table
- How to Use RetailFlow - 1 copy per table

**Reduces printing by ~40%**

---

### Option 3: Minimal Print (Budget/Sustainability Focus)

**Only print:**
1. Frameworks Reference Sheet (1 per participant)
2. AI Investment Checklist (1 per participant)
3. Personal Action Plan Worksheet (1 per participant)

**Everything else:** Display RetailFlow on projection screen, make available digitally on course website

**Total:** ~15 pages per participant
