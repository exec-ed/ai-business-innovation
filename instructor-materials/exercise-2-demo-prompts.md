## TECHNIQUE 1: RISK DEEP-DIVE
*AI finds threats you missed*

### Demo Prompt (30 seconds):
```
I'm planning to deploy AI chatbots for customer service in retail. What are 3 risks I might have overlooked?
```

### Alternative Prompt:
```
We're implementing dynamic pricing AI. What could go wrong that I haven't considered?
```

---

## TECHNIQUE 2: REVERSE PROMPTING
*AI asks YOU the questions*

### Demo Prompt (45 seconds):
```
I need to evaluate an AI project for my organization. Ask me 5 critical questions I should answer before investing.
```

### Alternative Prompt:
```
I'm considering AI inventory optimization for a retail chain. What questions should I be asking before making this investment?
```

---

## TECHNIQUE 3: AI DEBATE
*Strategic perspectives clash*

### VERSION 1: Two AIs Cross-Critique ⭐ MOST INTERACTIVE

**Step 1 - Ask BOTH AIs (ChatGPT + Claude):**
```
Should RetailFlow invest $1M in AI dynamic pricing now, or wait 18 months? Present your recommendation with 3 supporting arguments.
```

**Step 2 - Copy AI #1's answer, paste into AI #2:**
```
Another AI strategist gave this recommendation: [PASTE AI #1's ANSWER HERE]

Critique this recommendation. What are the 3 weakest points in their argument?
```

**Step 3 - Copy AI #2's critique, paste into AI #1:**
```
Another strategist critiqued my recommendation with these points: [PASTE AI #2's CRITIQUE HERE]

Respond to these criticisms. Where is their critique wrong or incomplete?
```

---

### VERSION 2: Two AIs, Assigned Sides

**AI #1 (ChatGPT) - PRO side:**
```
Argue FOR investing $1M in AI dynamic pricing NOW. Give 3 strong arguments supporting immediate investment.
```

**AI #2 (Claude) - CON side:**
```
Argue AGAINST investing in AI dynamic pricing now. Recommend waiting 18 months. Give 3 strong arguments for delaying investment.
```

**Then Cross-Critique:**
```
[In AI #2] Another strategist argued FOR immediate investment: [PASTE AI #1's ANSWER]

Critique their arguments. What are the 3 weakest points?
```

---

### VERSION 3: Single AI, Both Sides ⭐ EASIEST FOR DEMO

**Demo Prompt (90 seconds):**
```
Facilitate a strategic debate about RetailFlow's AI investment decision:

**Chief Innovation Officer (PRO):**
Argues we should invest $850K in AI dynamic pricing NOW.
Present 3 compelling arguments for immediate action.

**Chief Financial Officer (CON):**
Argues we should wait 18 months before investing.
Present 3 compelling arguments for delaying investment.

After presenting both sides, have each executive rebut the other's strongest argument.
```

### Simplified Version:
```
Debate: Should we invest in AI chatbots now or wait 18 months?

Present two perspectives:
- VP Innovation: Argues for NOW (3 reasons)
- CFO: Argues for WAIT (3 reasons)

Each rebuts the other's top argument.
```

## TIPS FOR LIVE DEMO

**Before the session:**
- Have browser tabs open: ChatGPT, Claude, Google Gemini
- Test prompts to know what to expect
- Bookmark this document for quick access

**During the demo:**
- Use Google Gemini (free, no login) for participant exercises
- Use ChatGPT + Claude for your cross-critique demo (shows different AI "personalities")
- Read prompts aloud as you paste them
- Narrate what you're doing: "I'm copying this answer... pasting into the other AI... now asking it to critique..."

**Key teaching moment:**
"Notice how each AI has blind spots. The debate reveals assumptions you wouldn't see with just one AI. This is why AI augments, not replaces, strategic thinking."

## RETAILFLOW-SPECIFIC PROMPTS
*Use the case study context participants already know*

### Risk Deep-Dive:
```
RetailFlow (500 employees, $500M revenue, 75 stores) is considering AI chatbots for customer service ($450K investment). What are 3 risks specific to mid-sized retail that we might overlook?

```
I'm the CEO of RetailFlow evaluating 4 AI investments: chatbots, dynamic pricing, inventory optimization, and fraud detection. Ask me 5 questions to determine which should be our priority.

Each rebuts the other's top concern.
