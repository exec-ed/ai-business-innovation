# Image Generation Prompts for Slide Deck
**Based on "ugly sketches" visual metaphor approach**

---

## Approach: Visual Metaphors for Strategic Concepts

**Philosophy from ugly_sketches.txt:**
- Use simple, concrete visual metaphors (not complex diagrams)
- Deliberately rough/sketchy style (signals "work in progress," invites participation)
- One clear metaphor per concept (simpler than the idea it represents)
- Create visual variation (slight changes show evolution/comparison)

**Style for ALL images:**
- Hand-drawn sketch style (black ink on white, loose lines)
- Simple shapes and stick figures
- Minimal text (labels only)
- Deliberately imperfect (not polished)
- Could be drawn on a napkin or whiteboard

## IMPLEMENTATION GUIDE

### How to Generate These Images

**Option 1: AI Image Generation (DALL-E, Midjourney, etc.)**
- Copy prompts above
- Add to each: "Simple hand-drawn sketch in black ink on white background, sketchy style like whiteboard drawing, minimal detail, could be drawn with marker pen"
- Generate, download, insert into slides

**Option 2: Commission Artist**
- Hire illustrator for consistent style
- Provide all prompts
- Request: Black and white, sketch style, simple shapes

**Option 3: Draw Yourself (Most Authentic)**
- Literally sketch on paper or whiteboard
- Photograph/scan
- Embrace imperfection (that's the point!)

### Inserting into Quarto Slides

After generating images, save as:
```
slides/images/
├── 01-title-crossroads.png
├── 02-traditional-vs-ai.png
├── 03-data-foundation.png
├── 04-data-thermometer.png
[etc...]
```

In slide-deck.qmd, insert:
```markdown
## Slide Title

![](images/01-title-crossroads.png){width=60%}

Bullet points here
```

### Style Consistency Rules

**All images should:**
- ✅ Black ink on white background (or very light gray)
- ✅ Hand-drawn aesthetic (loose, imperfect lines)
- ✅ Stick figures for people (not detailed)
- ✅ Simple geometric shapes (boxes, circles, triangles)
- ✅ Minimal text (labels only, not paragraphs)
- ✅ One clear concept per image (not cluttered)
- ✅ Could plausibly be drawn on whiteboard in 2 minutes

**Avoid:**
- ❌ Polished, professional graphics (defeats "ugly sketch" purpose)
- ❌ Colour (except minimal use for emphasis: red for danger, green for good)
- ❌ Complex details (keep it simple)
- ❌ Realistic illustrations (stick figures > realistic people)
- ❌ Corporate clip art aesthetic

---

## Why This Approach Works

**From ugly_sketches.txt wisdom:**

1. **"Low perceived finishedness"** - Rough sketches signal "work in progress," inviting participation
2. **Visual metaphors** - Make abstract concepts concrete and memorable
3. **Simple > Complex** - One clear idea, easy to grasp in 2 seconds
4. **Variations** - Slight changes across similar visuals create connections

**Applied to your course:**
- Executives see rough sketches → "This is a working session, not a lecture"
- Visual metaphors stick → "Oh right, the building-on-foundation image"
- Simple visuals → Quick comprehension, no cognitive overload
- Consistent style → Professional but approachable
