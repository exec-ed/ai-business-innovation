## ⚠️ CONCERNS & SUGGESTIONS

### CONCERN 1: Opening is Too Rushed
**Current: 9:00-9:15 (15 min)**
- Welcome (5 min)
- Leadership Assessment (10 min)
- Roundtable sharing (?)

**Problem:**
- Leadership Assessment takes 5-10 minutes
- Roundtable for 15-20 people = 15-20 minutes
- You're already 10-15 min behind by 9:15

**SUGGESTION: Expand opening to 25 minutes**
```
9:00-9:10 (10 min): Welcome + Start Assessment
  - Welcome (3 min)
  - "While I set up, take the Leadership Assessment" (7 min)
  - They do it on phones/laptops

9:10-9:25 (15 min): Assessment Results + Roundtable
  - Quick review of 4 leadership styles (5 min)
  - Roundtable: "What style are you?" (10 min, 30 sec each)

9:25-9:55 (30 min): What Makes AI Different
  - Now properly paced, not rushed
```

**Impact:** Better pacing, people aren't stressed from minute 1

**Problem:**
- If Dragon's Den runs long (it will), Exercise 4 gets cut
- Strategic Scenarios is valuable practice

**SUGGESTION: Different approach**

**OPTION B: Integrate into Morning Break**
