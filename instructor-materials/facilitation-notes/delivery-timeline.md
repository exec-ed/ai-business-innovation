# AI-Driven Business Innovation - One Day Masterclass

## Course Overview
**Duration**: 9:00 AM - 4:30 PM (7.5 hours including breaks)
**Audience**: Executives, strategists, innovation leaders, portfolio managers
**Style**: Strategic simulation with AI-specific investment frameworks
**Goal**: Learn to evaluate AI initiatives using BOTH traditional AND AI-specific criteria

**What makes this AI-specific:**
- Data readiness assessment before investment
- Continuous learning costs and plans
- Accuracy vs. risk tolerance matching
- Explainability requirements
- Ethical risk and bias evaluation

## NEW: AI-Specific Evaluation Criteria

Every AI investment must satisfy BOTH:

### A. Traditional Investment Criteria
- Strategic fit (Three Horizons, Transformation Matrix)
- Financial ROI (cost reduction, revenue growth, risk, strategic)
- Implementation feasibility

### B. AI-Specific Investment Criteria (NEW)
1. **Data Readiness** (0-10 score, need 7+)
2. **Continuous Learning Plan** (30-50% ongoing costs)
3. **Accuracy vs. Risk Tolerance** (match requirements to capability)
4. **Explainability Requirements** (regulatory, operational, customer)
5. **Ethical Risk Assessment** (bias testing, diverse teams, monitoring)

**Decision rule**: Must pass BOTH sets of criteria
