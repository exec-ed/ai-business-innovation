## 📚 REQUIRED PRE-READINGS (~37 minutes)

Please read these **three documents** in order:

### 1. What Executives Need to Know About AI (12 minutes)
**Foundation:** What is AI? What types matter for business? What's your data maturity?

**Key insight:** AI success depends on data maturity—match your AI ambitions to your data reality.

### 2. Strategic AI Investment: A Portfolio Approach (15 minutes)
**Strategy:** Where should you invest? How do you balance your AI portfolio?

**Key insight:** Use the 70/20/10 rule across three time horizons—portfolio balance matters more than individual project ROI.

### 3. Why AI Innovation is Different (10 minutes)
**Evaluation:** What makes AI different from traditional IT? How do you assess if an AI project is viable?

**Key insight:** Traditional criteria aren't enough—add five AI-specific criteria (data, learning, accuracy, explainability, ethics).

**📎 All three readings are attached to this email.**

**Why three readings?** You need all three perspectives for tomorrow's exercises:
- **Foundation** helps you understand AI capabilities and limitations
- **Strategy** helps you allocate budget across competing initiatives
- **Evaluation** helps you identify which projects will succeed vs. fail

Together, they answer: *What is AI? Where should I invest? How do I evaluate viability?*

## 🎯 THE LEARNING APPROACH

We'll use **RetailFlow Inc.** (a fictional mid-market retail company) as the case study for all group exercises.

**Why a case study?**
- Practice frameworks in a safe environment without IP concerns or company politics
- Everyone analyzes the same information, enabling rich debate
- Focus on decision-making frameworks, not your organisational constraints

**How it works:**
1. **Morning:** Learn frameworks and practice on RetailFlow
2. **Afternoon:** Apply to RetailFlow in a 90-minute "Dragon's Den" investment simulation
3. **End of day:** Translate lessons to YOUR organisation in a Personal Action Plan

You'll receive all RetailFlow materials during the course—**no advance preparation needed beyond the three pre-readings.**

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

## 📊 WHAT YOU'LL LEARN

Five strategic frameworks for AI investment decisions:

1. **AI Transformation Matrix** - Classify initiatives by strategic focus and magnitude
2. **Data Value Pyramid** - Assess organisational AI readiness and data maturity
3. **Three Horizons Model** - Balance AI portfolio across timeframes (70/20/10 rule)
4. **AI Investment Model** - Calculate ROI across four value categories
5. **Innovation Adoption Framework** - Plan your phased transformation journey

**Centerpiece Exercise: Dragon's Den (90 minutes)**

You'll role-play as either an investment committee or project team, evaluating RetailFlow's four competing AI proposals:
- AI Customer Service Chatbot ($525K)
- Dynamic Pricing Optimization ($1.3M)
- AI Inventory Optimization ($1.25M)
- Fraud Detection System ($1.05M)

**The challenge:** $2M budget, but $2.95M in proposals. You cannot fund everything.

You'll apply both traditional ROI analysis AND AI-specific criteria to make tough portfolio decisions—just like you do in real life.
